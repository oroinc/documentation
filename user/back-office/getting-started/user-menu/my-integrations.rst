My Integrations
===============

.. note:: This feature is available in the OroCommerce Enterprise Edition.

.. hint:: This section is part of the :ref:`Integration Flows Concept Guide <concept-guide--integration-flows>` topic that provides a general understanding of Activepieces integration flows, how they work with OroCommerce, and the business processes that they can automate.

**My Integrations** opens your personal workspace in |Activepieces|, a third-party automation platform that is embedded in the OroCommerce back-office. In this workspace, you can build and manage your own integration flows. For example, you can create a flow that sends you a notification when a record is assigned to you.

The flows that you create in **My Integrations** run on your behalf and with your permissions. Flows that are shared by the whole team are available in :ref:`System Flows <user-guide--integrations--activepieces--system-flows>`.

.. note:: **My Integrations** is available when an administrator has enabled :ref:`Integration Flow Designer <admin-configuration-integration-flow-designer-settings>` and has granted your role the **My Integrations** permission.

To reach **My Integrations**:

1. Click on your user name at the top right of the application page.
2. Click **My Integrations**.

.. image:: /user/img/system/integrations/activepieces/my-user-my-integrations.png
   :alt: The My Integrations page

The first time you open **My Integrations**, the application creates your Activepieces user. If an administrator has enabled the **Enable Connection Autocreation For Users** setting, the application also creates the connection to OroCommerce automatically. As a result, you can create flows immediately, without connecting Activepieces to OroCommerce manually.

From the **My Integrations** page, you can:

* :ref:`Create a flow <user-guide--integrations--activepieces--create-flow>`
* :ref:`Review the executions of your flows and manage your connections <user-guide--integrations--activepieces--workspace>`
